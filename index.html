The Problem

Your product is good.
Your retention is killing you.

Most SaaS founders obsess over acquisition. But the real money — and the real danger — lives in what happens after the sale.

01

No onboarding system

Your new customers get a welcome email and then silence. By week 3, half of them have stopped logging in. By month 2, they're gone — and you never saw it coming.

02
